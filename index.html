What we do

Full yard maintenance, start to finish.

Everything that keeps a property looking cared for, on a schedule you never have to chase.

Weekly & biweekly mowing

Cut at the right height for the season so the lawn holds colour instead of burning off. Straight lines, no scalped corners.

Edging & trimming

Crisp lines along drives, walks and beds. String-trimmed around fences, posts and anywhere the mower can’t reach.

Blowing & cleanup

Drives, walks and porches blown clean before we leave. You should not be able to tell where the truck parked.

Mulch spreading

Laid even at the right depth and pulled back off the stems, so beds look sharp and the plants stay healthy.

Bush & hedge trimming

Shrubs shaped and thinned on a schedule that keeps them full, instead of letting them go woody and bare at the base.

Weeding & bed upkeep

Beds pulled by hand and kept that way, so you’re not looking at the same weeds again in two weeks.

What we don’t do: patios, retaining walls and hardscape. We’d rather do lawns properly than do everything halfway — and if you need that work, we’ll tell you who to call.
